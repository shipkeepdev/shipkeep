# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Use GitHub private vulnerability reporting: open the "Security" tab of the repository, then "Report a vulnerability".

Please include affected version, impact, reproduction steps and any proof of concept.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 3 business days.
- Initial assessment within 10 business days.
- Fix or mitigation for critical and high issues as fast as possible, with a coordinated disclosure date agreed with the reporter.
- Public disclosure 90 days after the report at the latest, or earlier once a fix is released.
- Credit in the release notes unless the reporter prefers to stay anonymous.

## Scope

The control plane, the agent, the CLI, the installer and official release artifacts. Applications deployed by users are out of scope.
