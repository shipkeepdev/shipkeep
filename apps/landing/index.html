No inbound port for the agent, no stored SSH key. Every agent opens its own outbound mTLS stream.

On any server

VPS, bare metal or Raspberry Pi. Linux and systemd are enough; the prerelease is tested on Ubuntu 24.04.

Truly multi-node

Placement and health checks. Target: failover in under 90 seconds when a server goes down.

No Kubernetes

Docker, Caddy, WireGuard and a Go agent under 50 MB.

Fits your existing workflow

Your CI builds the image, Shipkeep deploys it, from any registry. No proprietary tooling.

One control plane

Apps and servers for all your clients, isolated per organization and environment.

Secure by default

mTLS, encrypted secrets, a confirmation for every destructive action, and an audit log.

Everything from the terminal

The CLI deploys, rolls back, and manages secrets and servers.

$ shipkeep deploy
pull 12 s · release 4 s
health 2/2 healthy
✓ live in 41 s
$ shipkeep rollback
✓ rolled back in 6 s

Keep the keys.
